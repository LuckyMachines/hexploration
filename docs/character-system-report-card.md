## A bar, stricter

- A player can identify character, role, and condition in one second at 64 px without relying on color.
- One stable `characterId` and `roleId` drives every product surface.
- Four-player sessions never repeat a character unless duplication is explicitly selected.
- State art cannot be approved without proving that it depicts the canonical person, costume, equipment, proportions, and visual scale.
- A changed neutral design invalidates dependent variants until they are reviewed again.
- Automated checks verify schema, asset presence, coverage, identity evidence, thumbnail legibility, and runtime resolution.
- The cutout aesthetic remains intentional and robust at every supported board camera angle.
